BIPs

Bitcoin Improvement Pre-Proposals. These are not numbered BIPs and have not been submitted to the BIP process.

  • Static Per-Output Miner Fee

    Pre-Proposal · Created July 23, 2026

    Bitcoin Improvement Pre-Proposal: a consensus-enforced fixed satoshi fee to miners for every new non-coinbase output, pricing permanent UTXO-creation costs that relay policy cannot bind.

  • Dynamic Escalation of the Per-Output Miner Fee

    Pre-Proposal · Created July 23, 2026

    Depends on Static Per-Output Miner Fee

    Bitcoin Improvement Pre-Proposal: optional EMA and 25th-percentile fee escalation on top of the static per-output miner fee so the deterrent does not decay over decades.
